# Ad mode

Advertising is its own genre: short, verb-first, addressed to the reader, ending in an action. Some patterns that give a post away are the working tools of an ad, so ad mode relaxes them. It does not relax the traces of generation, and it adds checks that only matter in ads.

Lint with `--ad`. The procedure in SKILL.md applies unchanged; only the rules below differ.

## Relaxed (SOFT in ad mode)

- **Audience call-outs**: "For anyone who runs every day", «Для тех, кто бегает каждый день», "Whether you're a parent or a night owl". Addressing a segment is what an ad does. Keep one, and make it specific.
- **Engagement prompts and calls to action**: "Tell us below", «Подписывайтесь», «Пишите в директ». An ad is allowed to ask. One call to action per ad.
- **Rhetorical hooks**: "Tired of tangled cables?", «Задумывались ли вы…». Fine as the first line when the next line answers with a fact.
- **Slogan fragments**: "Fresh. Local. Yours." Allowed, but they must still say something; three adjectives that fit any product say nothing.

Intensifier piles stay SOFT as in standard mode. In a real customer quote they are the customer's voice; in the seller's own lines they are filler.

## Still HARD

- **Dashes.** Besides being a tell, a dash breaks badly in narrow layouts and all-caps banners: it can start a line or hang alone.
- **Emphasis quotes**, staged reversals, staged reveals, recap markers, stock scene-setting.
- **Hype vocabulary**: revolutionize the way, unlock your potential, take it to the next level, game-changer, «раскройте потенциал», «на новый уровень», «погрузитесь в атмосферу». In an ad these replace the benefit the reader came for.
- **The assistant frame**: an ad is not a reply to a user.
- **Invented facts**: ratings, testimonials, customer counts, awards, scarcity. Never.

## Added in ad mode (SOFT)

- **Claims that need proof**: best, #1, number one, leading, guaranteed, risk-free, 100%; «лучший», «№1», «лидер рынка», «самый выгодный», «гарантия», «100%». Advertising rules in many jurisdictions and most ad platforms require such claims to be substantiated. Keep one only with proof you can show (a test, a rating with its source, a stated guarantee); otherwise make it concrete ("ships in two days").
- **Urgency**: only today, last chance, hurry, limited time, don't miss out; «только сегодня», «последний шанс», «успейте», «осталось всего», «количество ограничено». Keep it only if true and state the real date or quantity.
- **Vague calls to action**: click here, learn more; «нажмите здесь», «подробнее по ссылке». Say what happens after the click: "See this week's menu", «Записаться на четверг».

## Ad checklist

- [ ] One offer and one call to action.
- [ ] The first line works alone; feeds and search results cut the rest. Check the platform's current length limits, they change.
- [ ] The benefit is concrete and comes from the source: a number, a time, a visible result.
- [ ] Price, terms and conditions are stated as the source states them, in the currency and format the local rules require (some jurisdictions require prices in the national currency).
- [ ] The reader's words, not the seller's: "your trainers come back white", not "premium cleaning solutions".
- [ ] No testimonial, rating, count or scarcity that the source does not contain.
- [ ] Read it at the size and case it will be shown in: line breaks, all caps, a mobile screen.

## Register pass, last

After the gate, tune the register for the medium: shorter sentences, verbs first, second person, concrete nouns, one idea per line. This pass runs last because it changes phrasing; running the gate after it would flatten the ad again.
