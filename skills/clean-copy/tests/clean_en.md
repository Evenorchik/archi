My rear brake started squealing after last week's rain, loud enough that people turned around at crossings. I assumed the pads were worn out, but they still had about 3 mm left.

The fix took twenty minutes. I took the wheel off, wiped the rim with isopropyl alcohol, and sanded each pad with 120-grit paper until the glazed surface looked matte again. Then I angled the pads in slightly, so the front edge touches the rim first. A folded business card between the back of the pad and the rim gives about the right gap.

Two rides later it is still quiet. If the noise comes back I will replace the pads, since a pair costs less than lunch.

One thing I did not expect: the left pad squealed more, and it turned out to be mounted a couple of millimetres lower than the right one. Check that before you buy anything.
